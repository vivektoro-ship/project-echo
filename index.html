Echo — partner guide

Plain language

Reporting an issue

You face an issue during a visit. What do you do?

Tap the orange flag at the bottom of the screen.

Do you have to type the visit details?

No. The app attaches them. You only describe the issue.

You are not inside a visit. Can you still report?

Yes. Open My Reports and tap Report an issue. Then search the visit ID.

What do you fill in?

Select the issue type. Describe what happened. Attach a photo if it helps. Tap Submit.

It says this visit is already reported. Why?

Another partner has already reported this visit. Tap View report. Do not submit again.

Can you complete it later?

Yes. It is saved as a Draft in My Reports.

After you submit

What happens next?

The support team receives it with all your details. You get a report number.

Where do you see your reports?

In My Reports. All your reports are listed there.

What does each status mean?

  • Reported — the support team has received it.
  • Being looked at — the support team is working on it.
  • Awaiting your reply — the support team needs one detail from you.
  • Resolved — the issue is closed and fixed.
  • Cancelled — the report was closed. The reason is shown inside.

Will you see who is handling it?

No. You will see only the status.

If it is taking time

Can you follow up?

Yes. Open the report and tap Nudge.

Why is Nudge unavailable?

You have used it recently. It becomes available again after a short wait.

The status says Awaiting your reply. What do you do?

Open the report. Read the question. Type your answer. Tap Send reply.

Can you keep replying after that?

No. It is one question and one reply. Give a complete answer.

Closing

How do you know it is done?

The status changes to Resolved. The details of the fix are shown inside.

Do you close the report?

No. The support team closes it.

For partner leads

How do you see your team's issues?

Open the same list. Use the tabs — In progress, Resolved, All.

Can you narrow the list?

Yes. Filter by city, or search by visit ID or partner name.

Points to remember

  • One issue, one report.
  • Describe what happened, not the cause.
  • A photo helps the support team resolve it faster.
  • Check My Reports first. No need to call.
